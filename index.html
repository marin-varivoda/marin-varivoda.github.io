Marin Varivoda

I am a PhD student and a teaching assistant at the Department of Mathematics, University of Zagreb. My advisor is Filip Najman.

I'm interested in number theory and arithmetic geometry, particularly elliptic curves and modular curves. I have also worked on convex geometry and the Banach–Mazur distance.
